## 2. Description of Services

LuminoLearn provides structured educational programs and a secure learning platform for children and youth ages 9 to 17. Our Services include:

* **Free consultations:** A complimentary 15-minute consultation with a Canadian educator, including a placement diagnostic to understand your child's starting point and recommend next steps.
* **Structured programs in three subjects:** Artificial Intelligence, Cybersecurity (including age-appropriate Post-Quantum Cryptography), and Math + Physics.
* **Three levels per subject:** Foundations (roughly ages 9 to 11), Building (roughly ages 12 to 14), and Depth (roughly ages 15 to 17). Placement within a level is based on the diagnostic and educator recommendation, not age alone.
* **Three instruction formats:**
  * One-on-one online
  * One-on-one in-person (currently available in the Greater Toronto Area)
  * Small-group online with up to six students matched by level and goals
* **Combined-course enrollment:** Optional enrolment across all three subjects for families who want a coordinated learning plan.
* **The LuminoLearn learning platform** at [app.luminolearn.ca](https://app.luminolearn.ca), where students access assigned practice, progress information, and communication with their educator, and where parents access the parent portal for progress review and account management.

Program details, scheduling, and format availability may vary by subject, level, and season. Specific arrangements for your family are confirmed in your enrollment confirmation.

## 4. Enrollment Process

### 4.1 Consultation and diagnostic

Families typically begin with a free 15-minute consultation with a Canadian educator, including a placement diagnostic. This conversation covers your child's strengths, goals, and whether LuminoLearn is a good fit. There is no obligation to enroll.

### 4.2 Personalized recommendation

Following the consultation, LuminoLearn provides a personalized recommendation covering format, subject(s), and level per subject based on the diagnostic and your family's goals.

### 4.3 Enrollment confirmation

When you choose to proceed, LuminoLearn issues an enrollment confirmation that documents the recommended plan, scheduling, and **current applicable pricing** for that plan. Submission of an enrollment request is not a confirmed enrollment until LuminoLearn has issued this confirmation and any required payment or agreement steps are complete.

### 4.4 Account creation on the learning platform

After enrollment is confirmed, LuminoLearn creates accounts on the learning platform at [app.luminolearn.ca](https://app.luminolearn.ca):

* A **parent account** is created first.
* A **student account** is created and linked to the parent account.

Login credentials are provided to the Parent. Parents are responsible for safekeeping credentials and supervising platform use as age-appropriate.

### 4.5 Parental consent for students under 13

For students under 13, an additional parental-consent step is completed before the student account is activated. This step verifies that a parent or guardian has approved the student's participation and platform access in line with Canadian privacy requirements for minors.

### 4.6 Right to decline enrollment

LuminoLearn reserves the right to decline an enrollment request if we determine that our programs are not an appropriate fit for a Student's needs, if enrollment capacity has been reached, or for other reasonable educational or operational reasons. In such cases, we will explain our reasoning and, where possible, suggest alternative options.

Students are expected to participate respectfully in all classes and platform interactions, treating educators and fellow students with courtesy. LuminoLearn maintains a safe, supportive learning environment. Behaviour that is persistently disruptive, disrespectful, or harmful to others may result in the following steps:

1. A conversation between the educator and the Parent to address the behaviour.
2. A temporary pause in classes or platform access to allow the situation to be resolved.
3. In rare cases, discontinuation of enrollment, with a prorated refund for remaining scheduled time where applicable.

We approach behavioural concerns with care and always involve Parents before taking action.

### 7.3 Academic integrity

Because AI is a subject we teach, students learn extensively about when and how to use AI tools thoughtfully. When concerns arise, we address them as learning opportunities in partnership with parents.
